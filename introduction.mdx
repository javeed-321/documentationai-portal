**OM Plus Delivery Manager (DM)** is Plus Technologies' enterprise output management platform. It streamlines print operations and enables advanced document delivery — routing every request to the right destination across network printers, local devices, server-to-server links, and electronic channels such as email and fax.

This guide is the administration and configuration reference for OM Plus DM. It walks through managing **destinations**, tuning the **system**, configuring the **client** interface, and setting up **security** — the day-to-day tasks an OM Plus administrator performs.

<Callout kind="info">
  This guide covers OM Plus DM administration across four areas — Destinations, System, Client, and Security. Use the **OM Plus DM** tab to move between chapters, or start from the cards below.
</Callout>
